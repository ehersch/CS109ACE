Welcome to 109ACE!

CS 109ACE is the ACE section for CS 109. We meet in a small group to review course concepts, ask questions, and work on practice problems. We’ll follow the main course through counting and probability, random variables and expected value, and introductory statistics and machine learning.

ACE is here to support your work in CS 109, without adding another set of assignments or exams. Our pace and practice will adapt to what you find useful. There will be additional office hours for ACE students, as well as a midterm and final review session. There will also be snacks!

Enrollment: Complete the ACE application form by Sunday, September 27 at 11:59 p.m. See ACE enrollment information for more details.
